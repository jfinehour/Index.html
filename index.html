NFL DFS · DraftKings & FanDuel

Build lineups. Then stress-test them.

Gridlock builds NFL lineups from your projections, then replays the slate thousands of times against a realistic contest field to show which lineups actually cash, which stacks carry them, and where the field is leaving value on the table. Everything runs on your device.

You can also drop a projections CSV anywhere on the page. Pick DK Classic, FD Classic or DK Showdown at the top first.

OptimizerProjection, Ceiling, Small Field and Large Field builds with stacks, bring-backs, locks, excludes and per-player min/max exposure.
SimulateReplay the slate against a synthetic field in several contests at once: cash rate, top 1%, win rate and ROI for every lineup.
Sim-builtLet the simulation search for the lineups with the best results, then re-score them on fresh replays so the numbers aren't flattered by luck.
Players & sim optimalsEdit any projection or ownership, see who lands in the best lineup of each replay, and where the field is underweight.
